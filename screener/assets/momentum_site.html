Momentum strategy study

Sixteen strategies from the cross-sectional, dual, time-series and long-only trend momentum literatures, backtested long-only over point-in-time Nifty 500 and S&P 500 membership. Every run charges costs and slippage. None of them short, lever, or earn interest on idle cash, so they read below the published figures by construction. Research, not financial advice.

Position sizing compounds. Each slot is sized from current realized equity, so the book stays invested as it grows. An earlier version of this study froze the slot at day-one capital, which left a compounding run mostly in idle cash and understated both its return and its drawdown by roughly half on the ten-year window. Those numbers are not comparable with these; treat any figure quoted before this note as superseded.
